Born In Bombay (1994)

Preserving The Soul Of Mumbai Street Food

What started as a single wooden pushcart near Girgaon Chowpatty is today an artisan culinary haven serving millions of authentic Vada Pavs without compromising original recipes.

Our Culinary Craft

The Anatomy of an Authentic Batata Vada

A true Mumbai Vada Pav isn't just fried potato inside bread—it's a delicate harmony of temperature, texture, and aroma.

Our potatoes are boiled to fluffy perfection, tossed with hand-pounded ginger, green chili paste, fresh curry leaves, and a fragrant mustard seed tadka. Each vada is hand-shaped, dipped in light chickpea batter, and flash-fried to golden perfection.

Served burning hot inside custom pillowy pav baked daily in our stone hearths.
